After 15 years in the industry:

The amount of BS you deal with is a linear function of how close you are to doing work that matters to the people running the show. Find work that matters to the people in charge, and the BS disappears.

The amount of money you get offered is more a function of how much margin the industry has than of the difficulty or value of the work you do.

For instance, bricks-and-mortar retail has low startup costs, and almost no margin, leading to constant cost-cutting, low salaries, and only the absolutely essential staying-afloat work being done.

On the other hand, insurance, banking, real estate advertising have massive startup costs and incredibly fat margins, leading to high salaries and many employees, most of whom are doing work that doesn't matter.

Neither extreme is pleasant to work at.
