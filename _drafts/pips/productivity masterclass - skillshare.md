- "i don't have time" - my time is in my control, i can do whatever i want, if i dont do something it's because i'm choosing not to do it, we are limited by the choices we make with our time

3 myths of productivity - myth 2 - motivation
myth of motivation
- motivation is a feeling - feelings are temporary, unreliable
- when you have discipline you go directly from thought to action
- we dont need to feel like doing something to do what you have to do
- [[Inside the mind of a master procrastinator - Tim Urban]]
- [[Screw motivation, what you need is discipline]]
- short term pain, long term useful -> usually requires motivation
- action, outcome -> long feedback loop
- [[Motivation Myth - Jeff Haden]]

- focusing on the action
	- make action more pleasurable, more likely to do the thing
	- make the consequences of inaction more painful
		- put money on the line
- focusing on the outcome
	- shorten the feedback loop
	- making the outcome more clear in your mind

journalling prompts
- what's a goal i want to achieve?
- how can i make the process more pleasureable
- can i increase my odds of hitting the goal by putting money on the line
- how can i make the outcomes more tangible and desirable
