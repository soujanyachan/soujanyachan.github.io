## productivity equation
- how much work we can get done in the smallest amount of time possible
- should be productive about the right things
- fun factor

```productivity = (useful output / time) x f
```

mental model - pilot, plane, engineer
- pilot - figures out the direction (10%) {USEFUL}
- plane - follows the course, takes off, lands, follows instruction (80%) {OUTPUT}
- engineer - makes sure plane is efficient, fuel efficiency, organise system (5-10%) {TIME}

which do i struggle with most of all pilot, plane engg ? in what specific ways?

## 3 myths of productivity - myth 1 - "i don't have time"
- "i don't have time" - my time is in my control, i can do whatever i want, if i dont do something it's because i'm choosing not to do it, we are limited by the choices we make with our time
- write down exactly what you are doing with your time for a few days / a week
- Make time - by Jake knapp -> book rec
- time is like a muscle, the more you use it, the more you can efficient you can be with it

journalling prompt 2
- what am i avoiding with the phrase "i dont have time"
- track your time for a week, literally everything

## 3 myths of productivity - myth 2 - motivation
myth of motivation
- motivation is a feeling - feelings are temporary, unreliable
- when you have discipline you go directly from thought to action
- we dont need to feel like doing something to do what you have to do
- [[Inside the mind of a master procrastinator - Tim Urban]]
- [[Screw motivation, what you need is discipline]]
- short term pain, long term useful -> usually requires motivation
- action, outcome -> long feedback loop
- [[Motivation Myth - Jeff Haden]]

- focusing on the action
	- make action more pleasurable, more likely to do the thing
	- make the consequences of inaction more painful
		- put money on the line
- focusing on the outcome
	- shorten the feedback loop
	- making the outcome more clear in your mind

journalling prompts
- what's a goal i want to achieve?
- how can i make the process more pleasureable
- can i increase my odds of hitting the goal by putting money on the line
- how can i make the outcomes more tangible and desirable

## myth of multitasking
- task switching
- attention residue
- flow state
	- when we voluntarily engaged in a task that's using all our attention, just difficult enough to be interesting but no so much that it is frustrating
	- stretch zone, panic zone
	- if we allow ourselves to be distracted we cant go into the flow state
	- i am welcoming the distraction because i'm not changing my behaviour

prompts
- what's one or more situations when i was in my flow state ?
- what circumstances and mindsets led to that
- can i manufacture those conditions for other stuff i need / want to do?

## parkinson's law
- work expands to fill the time allotted to it
- [[Zero to One - Peter Thiel]] - think about your ten year plan and how are you going to acheive it in the next 6 months?
- give yourself artifical deadlines - forcing you to prioritise

prompts
- make a list of 3-4 long term tasks you want to do
- what would you do if you only had half the time to do them
- what about if you had to do them in the next 24 hours ?

____

## pareto's law - 80/20 principle
- 80% of the output comes from 20% of the efforts
- [[4-hour workweek]]
- justinguitar.com
- continual process of 80/20 on whatever work is left

prompt
- what 20% of my work is driving 80% of my useful output
- what's taking up 80% of my time but not actually contributing much to my outcomes ?

## newton's first law of motion
- two-minute rule - if it takes this long then just do it instead of putting it on your to do list
- five-minute rule - if struggling to get started with anything, do five mins of it
- eliminate the activation energy
- [[Atomic Habits]]
- keystone habits - habit that paves the way for other habits / important work

prompt
do something you've been putting off

- how can i help make those habits stick?
