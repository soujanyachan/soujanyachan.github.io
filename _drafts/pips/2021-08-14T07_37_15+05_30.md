After reading and replying to a lot of other comments I just realized why this didn’t happen to me during my latter time in the real world. I had a simple heuristic:

1. Is the development, deployment, architecture, etc set up to handle most issues via simple rollbacks and do they have a fault tolerant, HA architecture. Also is everyone willing to take ownership of anything that comes up. 
2. If no, am I being hired to help them reach those goals and are the current employees receptive to those changes. 

If the answer to both of those questions are “no”, I won’t work there.
