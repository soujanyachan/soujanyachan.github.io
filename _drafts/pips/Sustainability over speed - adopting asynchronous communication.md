> Slack is designed to keep your attention within its confines, which sometimes comes at the expense of actually getting any work done

## 8 benefits of asynchronous communication
- mindful conversations - more time to shape your thoughts
- more transparency - due to knowledge base - https://about.gitlab.com/handbook/
- no FOMO, FOMO -> JOMO
- easier to get into flow 
- more autonomy
- timezone agnostic
- lower anxiety

## How to work asynchronously
- document everything
- internal knowledge wiki
- communication guide
- **Default to transparency.** Work discussions should happen via internal rather than private communication channels. Make your documents accessible to everyone. Don’t make it hard to access content you created.
- time blocking
- **meetings** Create an agenda, take notes, and share action items with full context after the meeting with everyone who was invited.
