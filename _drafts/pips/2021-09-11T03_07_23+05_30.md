Hobbies are mostly for people who are more financially well off or just simply have more time to pursue them without worrying about making ends meet. You wanna know the real killer of creativity? Stress. I am incidentally creative. I occasionally interior decorate and I like that. My wallpaper and bathroom right now look bomb. I enjoy putting funny pics of one of my co-workers on things and that’s fun. But a serious, cultivated skill? Takes time and money. I was killing the creativity I had by worrying about what I wasn’t doing, without thinking of how realistically it takes a lot of energy to work and pay bills and live. Obsessed with finding my ‘hobby’. We don’t live in a society designed to support the development of leisure activities.
