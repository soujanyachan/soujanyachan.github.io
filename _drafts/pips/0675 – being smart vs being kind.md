### What’s so good about being smart?
- **There is a certain intrinsic pleasure to knowing things.**
- **There’s a practical value to it.**
- **There’s also a social aspect to smartness.**

You start to avoid certain things – particularly areas that you’re not so sure about. You start picking your battles according to what’s winnable, rather than what’s most interesting or useful.

This is where we get to what separates the pros from the noobs. The smartest people embrace their ignorance. They are intimately familiar with the limitations of their models, and they are excited when they discover that they’re wrong about something.
