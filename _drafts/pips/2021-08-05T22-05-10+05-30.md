This. It's still possible to fall into a new hole, but overall, this approach leads to better jobs *for you* over time.

I typically make a "spider web graph" for each job I'm considering (including current).

Start with a point at the center of the graph, and add a new axis for each value you can identify, with the center being zero, and the edge being "best possible" -- for me that's 1, but it's pretty arbitrary

For each job, take a guess at how you think it performs against each metric, and plot it's position.

Examine the shape generated for each job, and make a decision from there.  Generally speaking, the largest, most circular shape "wins," but really this is a tool to help you identify what values are actually important to you

Edit: example axis labels: tech stack, culture, on-call, money, remote, location, team size
