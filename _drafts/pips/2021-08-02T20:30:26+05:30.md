Maybe...there may be too much history though.

Generally, leadership is about vision, villain and victory.  When we lose respect in a leader it's because they failed at the victory part.  Without success we start questioning the vision and then we start to reinterpret everything about the villain.

As someone else noted - the revenue seems low for the headcount.  That means people don't want to buy your solution as much as management/you believed they would.  Why is that?

You may be able to reconnect with the founder over drinks / lunch / coffee / 1:1 and just talking through all the things they learned.  Maybe the villain changed ( it went from solving world hunger to buying them a cool car or something ).  Maybe the victory was only possible through luck.  Maybe the vision just wasn't good enough once they tried to execute it and the market went "meh".  

Through those conversations you may get seduced by their vision all over again.  You may see a bigger play in a longer term that you weren't aware of.  Or you may just see a person who's trying hard to achieve something audacious.  Or, they may just be a narcissistic asshole and you're happy to nope out before things get bad.

Is that helpful?
