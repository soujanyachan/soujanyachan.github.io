non-fiction:

• the new jim crow: mass incarceration in the age of colorblindness by michelle alexander

super super super important to understand the prison industrial complex, war on drugs, and the language/rhetoric that allows it to persist

just mercy: a story of justice and redemption by bryan stevenson V important, about the death penalty and how unethical and racist it is and should not exist in

the first place

becoming by michelle obama

vagina problems by lara parker

if you know anyone who has vaginismus, endometriosis, vulvadynia, etc, all these issues that are common and almost NEVER talked about-read this book so you want to talk about race by ijeoma oluo

the spy and the traitor: the greatest espionage story of the cold war • they were her property: white women as slave owners in the american south by stephanie e jones-rogers

white women are often portrayed as "innocent" bystanders to their white supremacist husbands/men in their lives but this was not true back then and is not true now - very important to understand the role white women play in upholding these institutions

fearing the black body: the racial origins of fat phobia by sabrina strings "beauty" standards, body ideals, the diet/weight loss/fitness industries --ALL come back to

racism and that needs to be thoroughly understood in the conversation about ending ALL of

these things
