Usually the projects that I find most challenging fall into two categories: either they are technically challenging or they are integrationally challenging or both - by that I mean either they stretch my technical understanding or they help me become more team oriented by involving my ability to handle cross team development.
One of the most technically and integration ally challenging projects I have worked on is the integration of a webrtc based calling solution into a react based SPA for customer support and ticketing. This is technically challenging due to its breadth as I need to review and manage not only UI changes written in functional style react, but also our nodejs backend as well as an elastic search integration for search as well as integrating solutions with our voice team. This also involved multiple deployments using jenkins and Kubernetes and updating production mysql tables using Liquibase.

 the reason that it is technically challenging is that we needed to be very careful in the manner of implementation to prevent race conditions. Especially as the voice subsystem and the ticketing subsystem were being forced to work together through this feature. 
 
 I really enjoy being able to work with multiple technologies in this way and would love to do more of the same.
