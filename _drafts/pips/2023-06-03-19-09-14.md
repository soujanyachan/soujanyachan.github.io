I've worked a lot of much more "comfortable" jobs since that time, and I've had a lot of time to think about why that luggage-monkey job was so much better than nearly all of the intellectual gigs I've held.  So far, I think it boils down to a simple point:  there's no worse tyranny than being told how to think.  No matter how much luggage I lifted, I was always free with my thoughts.  But when you work in an industry where your *thoughts* are your primary output, it's inevitable that someone (or some*thing*) will try to consume *all* of them.  When that happens, you're on a path to burnout
