Self-authorship is the belief that you can rely on your own internal values to make decisions. It’s accepting the fact that you cannot control all the external event happening to you and around you, but you can control the way you react.

phases of self-authorship
![[https://nesslabs.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/self-authorship-illustration.png]]
- trust your internal voice - shape how you react
- internal foundation - conscious process where you decide to combine your identity, your relationships, your beliefs and your values into a set of internal commitments from which to act upon.
- internal commitments - making internal commitments to actually acting upon them.

activities to develop self-authorship
- question your values and beliefs
- hang out with diverse people - increase your awareness, understanding and openness to diversity, and at the same time develop your own values, which may be either inspired by these diverse experiences, or developed as counter values and beliefs.
- take responsibility for your learning experience and growth
