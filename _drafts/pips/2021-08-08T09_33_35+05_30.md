This may sound mean, but I always have an “out” for social gatherings. If I’m with my family for a weekend, I mention there was electrical problems on my block and my super might need my key. The visit is going poorly? “Oh no! That’s my super. I have to be back tonight for the electrician. Darn.” Same with friends. That way I know I can go see them and not lose those close to me but I also have boundaries if I need them. Sometimes it’s hard to enforce them so a previously explained excuse is a godsend.
