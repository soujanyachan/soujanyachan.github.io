we need to alternate between focus and distraction

pushing through distractions all the time can give us the illusion of productivity but often, productivity decreases 

taking breaks can
- restore motivation
- prevent decision fatigue
- consolidate memories
- increase creativity
- improve well-being

short breaks (5 to 10 minutes) every hour or so, and longer breaks (at least 30 minutes) every two to four hours.

activities
- movement
- meditation
- social
- nature
- creative - art, music
