## deciding what matters
- 90% or more important
- **every yes takes time away from another experience**
- regretted allowing the expectations of others to guide their life
- everything is not equally essential
- 90% rule - that's where the really essential activities are
- what tasks are clear yeses to you?

- what is something essential that you are under-investing in right now ?
	- why does it matter to you
	- what needs to change
	- how much time will it take
	- what does success look like in this area?

- create a visual representation of the progress
- maintain a routinized weekly checklist ot avoid wasting time scheduling essential tasks
