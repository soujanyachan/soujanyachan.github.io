# How to Organise your Workflow to Maximise Productivity
## pilot
- setting the intention for the rest of the day
- short term, medium term and long term
- horizons of focus - [[Getting Things Done]]
	- calendars and actions - ground level
	- projects - more than one action - horizon 1
	- areas of responsibility / goals for life - horizon 2
	- goals and general objectives - horizon 3
	- 3-5 year vision - horizon 4
	- principles and purpose - horizon 5

### scheduling and calendar
- [[Deep Work]]
- timeblocked and actually scheduled the day
- if we dont have a default thing in the calendar then you are not getting distracted from anything

### to-do lists
- might-do list -> all task
- to-do -> what to do today
- daily highlight -> something that has to get done
- 1 big task, 3 medium tasks, 5 big tasks

### projects and areas of focus
- project is anything that has more than one task associated with it
- a task needs to be immediately actionable and ideally have a deadline associated
- more specific task is, the easier it is to get it done
- every project needs to have a next action
- slow burn -> heavy lift vs slow burn of working on things
- if written down, brain can do background processing

### goals
- SMART goals ?
- journey before destination due to shifting goalpost
- types of goals
	- object oriented goal
	- progress goal
	- system goal
- some goals are fully within your control, don't let an externality define your success rather than internal processes
- use goal to set direction, but then forget the goal
- [[I've never had a goal - Jason Fried]]

### vision purpose
- odyssey plan
	- three q
		- in five years time what would my life look like if i was continuing down exactly the same path
		- what would life look like if different path
		- no money or societal expectations in 5 years time
- deathbead exercise
	- 1 year from now
	- 2, 5, 10 years
- physical health, mental health, relationships, financials

### achieving flow
- model of flow state
- flow is still a happy accident when it happens, we just need to make ourselves accident prone
- tips for getting into flow
	- external distraction
	- internal distraction - journalling / todo -> haven't externalised / meditation
	- one task at a time
	- mental cue - for entering in the flow state
	- choose challenging but not impossible tasks
	- work at your biological peak time
	- right music -> instrumental
	- consume caffeine (1-2 cups of coffee)

### distraction management
- not cool to be distracted / busy all the time
- more enjoyable and more meaningful to us
- add friction to distractions
- reduce friction from things you want to do
- d
	- rearrange apps on home screen
	- put social media in another folder
	- grayscale mode
