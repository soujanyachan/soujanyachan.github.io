What happens to a persistent volume when released from its claim.
Valid options are
- Retain (default for manually created PersistentVolumes),
- Delete (default for dynamically provisioned PersistentVolumes), and
- `"Delete"` means the volume will be deleted from Kubernetes on release from its claim.
- `"Recycle"` means the volume will be recycled back into the pool of unbound persistent volumes on release from its claim.
- `"Retain"` means the volume will be left in its current phase (Released) for manual reclamation by the administrator. The default policy is Retain.

When Kubernetes creates a Pod it assigns one of these QoS classes to the Pod:
- Guaranteed
    - every container in pod must have mem req and limit, cpu req and limit
    - mem limit = mem req, cpu limit = cpu req
- Burstable
    - not guaranteed
    - at least one container in pod has mem or cpu req
- BestEffort
    - no mem or cpu limit or req
