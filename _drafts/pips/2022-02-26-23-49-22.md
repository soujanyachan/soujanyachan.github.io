. Identify problem type (array traversal, tree traversal, tree construction, graph traversal, etc...)

 2. Frame the problem in those words to your interviewer, cutting away the fluff of the layman wording

 3. Think about common approaches you have seen to this problem type. Illuminate the approaches you are thinking about and ask for the interviewers opinion. Come to a consensus on the approach BEFORE you start coding

 4. Map out the solution in simple scaffolding/pseudo code, confirm this general approach with the interviewer and talk through the algorithm along the way.

 5. Code the solution, using a test case along the way

 6. Arrive at a good solution and talk about improvements and trade offs. After writing what you did how could it be improved as you look back.
