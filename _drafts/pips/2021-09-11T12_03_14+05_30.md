This is absolutely right. But there are a lot of issues at work here. Here's a few of them.

1. Geeks like to be geeks. They perceive it as giving them some sort of status. Which it certainly does, amongst their peers. But not with others. And it can very easily exclude people; that's a big problem for some.

2. Quite a lot of technical people only understand what they're dealing with at a certain level. Quite a few are actually incapable of thinking about a problem at a level that other people can understand. 3. For some, it's a mask. Their technical knowledge is actually fairly limited, but they know that if they use the right language nobody will question them about it.
