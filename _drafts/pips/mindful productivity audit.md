1.  What is the first thing I do in the morning?
2.  How do I manage my calendar?
	1. How many events are there in a typical week?
	2. How many events were created by me, versus invitations I received from other people?
	3. Could any of these meetings have been replaced by an email, a shared document, or another format?
4.  How much time do I dedicate to deep work during a typical day?
5.  How many breaks do I take during a typical day?
6.  How do I cultivate my curiosity?
7.  Do I have time for self-reflection during the week?
8.  Is my physical workspace setting me up for success?
9.  Is my digital workspace setting me up for success?
	1. What does your desktop currently look like?
	2. What apps are currently installed? Which ones do I regularly use?
	3. What tools do I use to manage my knowledge?
11.  How do I know the work day is over?
12.  What is the last thing I do in the evening?
