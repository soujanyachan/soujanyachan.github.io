identify your fixed mindset triggers
- **Having to work hard.** Most people like to say they’re hard workers. But in reality, there is something society seems to value more: being “gifted” or “naturally talented.” It just means you have a self-limiting belief—that you think that no matter how much work you put in, some people are just more talented than you are.
- **Facing setbacks.**
- **Getting negative feedback.**
- **Being challenged.**
- **Seeing success in others.**

Foster a growth mindset 
- remember neuroplasticity
- appreciate the process over the results
- acknowledge your weaknesses
- sense of purpose
- don't say failing, say learning / challenges as opportunities
- value effort over talent
- growth before speed
- don't chase other's approval
- view criticism as a gift
- celebrate actions, not attributes
- grow with others
- take the time to reflect 
- cultivate perseverance
- not yet - Carol Dweck says adding “not yet” to any fixed mindset statement is a great way to reframe your thought processes.

journaling for growth mindset
- 4 weekly planning questions
	- What am I struggling with right now?
	- What do I want to learn by the end of this week?
	- What is one thing I will try that will get me out of my comfort zone?
	- Who can I reach out to for help this week?
- 3 daily reflection questions
	- What did I do today that made me think hard?
	- What new strategies did I try?
	- What mistake did I make that taught me something?
