1) 6 Months expenses as Contingency Fund in FD (6 Fixed Deposit, each FD to cover one month expense).

2) Health Insurance of 5 Lakhs if Individual and 10 Lakhs for Husband-Wife and two kids (Minimum side of coverage). 

3) Term Insurance Plan (20 Times of Annual Salary as Sum Assured and coverage till your younger kid becomes 25 years) for bread earners.

4) Pension Fund: APY /NPS or start investing in an Index Fund on a regular basis for long term.

5) Direct plan (not regular) in Mutual Funds, and Stocks/Direct share market investments.

6) Invest your 10% of total Savings in the form of Gold (Preferably Sovereign Gold Bond/SGB).
