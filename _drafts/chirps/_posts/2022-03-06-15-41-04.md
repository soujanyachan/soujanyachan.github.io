intentional weight loss is inherently disordered, and the only reason people don't broadly see it as such is because we have normalized a pursuit of thinness at all costs at the societal level and get bombarded with messaging in that vein constantly from birth. 

choosing nutritious foods, working on listening to hunger cues, moving your body more-all great. good goals for everyone to work on! trying to force your body to be some specific size, not so much.
