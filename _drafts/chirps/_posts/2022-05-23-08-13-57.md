even habit forming requires some amount of work. asking yourself the question why am I stuck will generate a lot of logical answers, none of which will help. what helps is to go to the emotion at the root of the issue 
check how long this feeling is there. what changed? 

not what you should do vs what you care to do. should is extrinsic. what do you care about.

motivation fluctuates.
what dictates motivation? triggered by all sorts of things. capitalise when the motivation is there. sustainable and preservable. create those circumstances
