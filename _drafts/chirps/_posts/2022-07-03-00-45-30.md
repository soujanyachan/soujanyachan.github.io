If the first rule of IT is to always have an escape plan, the second is that you don't stick around with any employer for more than a year unless you genuinely like the job, feel you are learning something, or are being given something substantive (if not promotions and raises, then at least raises.) If that describes your situation, you update your resume, and start applying to anything that moves, doesn't require certs and degrees you don't have / need (so, don't apply to become a lawyer, but if you already have an IT job you probably don't need your A+, you know?) and you're at least 1/4th qualified for.
