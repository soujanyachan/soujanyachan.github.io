My basic vinegarette formula is usually some form of this:

Pick an acid (lemon juice, balsamic vinegar, red wine vinegar, etc)

Pick an oil (olive oil, canola oil, sunflower oil, etc)

Pick an onion (red, yellow, shallots, whatever you prefer)

Chop your onion and combine it with your acid. Let that sit for 15-20 minutes. Add twice the amount of oil as acid along with salt, pepper, and any other seasonings you want (garlic, honey, oregano, whatever it is you're feeling). I use my immersion blender to blend it at this point, but you can also put it in a jar and shake it or whisk it instead.
