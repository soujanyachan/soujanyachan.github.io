Watched Encanto. Halfway through the movie I knew it was a Lin Manuel Miranda music production for sure. Written in the style of a musical, it touches on generational trauma, living up to expectations, being the odd one out and family ties.
The music was all beautiful, heavily Colombian inspired and there was a whole song only in Spanish! 
There was no villain in the movie but the stakes were high enough to engross me throughout the movie. The animations especially of natural things were so colourful and beautiful. You can tell that there was a lot of love and effort behind this. 

Rating: 8.5/10
