On a podcast I listen to called Happier it was talking about [paraphrasing from memory] making life less mundane by trying to do one small and one large adventure every week. Small adventures an hour or two and large are a few hours or half a day. The idea is to break up the routine of life.

This week I am thinking of seeing a horror movie for the small adventure and taking a day trip to a town a couple hours away, maybe check out a coffee shop, restaurant, art space, scenic areas.  

That last idea also inspired by a book called Psalm for the Wild-Built “respite from urban life…It was a place designed for temporary use, somewhere you went to, soaked up, and left behind.”. 

As I type this I realize I also use podcasts, books and movies to break away from my phone.
