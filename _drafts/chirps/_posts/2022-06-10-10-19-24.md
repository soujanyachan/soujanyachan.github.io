Schedule all work in focus blocks (I do 1.5 hours, he did 1 hour because he has a lot of meetings)

 • During those focus blocks, respond to absolutely nothing. No emails, texts, slack messages, surprise meetings, nothing. Turn on do not disturb if you must.

 • Plan out your day in the mornings, and schedule 30 minutes every morning for you to do so. 

 • Schedule your focus blocks around “known” distractions (meetings, lunch, doctors apt, etc)

 • Be very selfish with your time. Don’t let someone schedule a meeting during your (pre-planned) focus block. If you have a gym routine, decline or reschedule all meetings that happen during that time. 

 • If you don’t have any known distractions, save the time in between your focus blocks for housekeeping items that don’t take much mental effort to do (for example: documentation, bugfixing, setting your schedule, responding to meetings) 

 • Take 15 minute breaks in between blocks to step outside and take a break

 • Do not schedule more than 4 blocks in a day
