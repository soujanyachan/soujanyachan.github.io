Pro tip to anyone going into an interview. When they ask "do you have any questions for us?" Say something along the lines of "no questions as such but just to be transparent I got an offer from another company for 5% more than this place, so I'll need a few days to make a decision." They ALWAYS come back and match the made up offer. If they ask which company say you signed an NDA in the interview process.
