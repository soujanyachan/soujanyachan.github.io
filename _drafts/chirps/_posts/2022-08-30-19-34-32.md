Thought loops
The food of the mind is thoughts. When mind needs to calm down the harder it tries to think

What does my mind avoid the most? It avoids boredom, I.e. No stimulus for the mind. People have the most negaive thoughts when they're bored.

A thought loop is structured as a series of if then statements to protect yourself.

Bullying your thought loops makes them come back harder

Become aware of the loop and the loop can deflate or go away 

Long term solution to the though tloop - what the mind wants to loop about is usually some insecurity, the underlying structure for the thought loop breaks down
