CLAY RECIPE

1 cup CORNSTARCH

1 cup PVA GLUE (/White School glue, eg.

https://amzn.to/3wRXq5L)

2 tbsp. BABY OIL (can be substituted with vegetable oil)

lemon juice)

1 tsp. WHITE VINEGAR (can be substituted with VASELINE

Steps:

1. Mix the top 4 ingredients in a microwave safe container.

2. Heat it in the microwave for 20 seconds.

3. Repeat 2-3 times, stirring in between.

4. Knead it for several minutes in your hands (with

a little cornstarch to prevent sticking).

5. Add a little vaseline to make the clay mixture silky smooth.

t should be stretchy and non-sticky at this point, and ready to sculpt.
