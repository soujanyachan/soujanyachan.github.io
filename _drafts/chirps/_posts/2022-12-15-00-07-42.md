stop wasting energy trying to find happiness at work and instead focus your energy on learning not to take your unhappiness from work home with you. A therapist may be useful to help you minimize the negative impact work has on your ability to derive joy from hobbies and “real life”. For me, radically accepting that work would not be the thing that makes me happy or fulfilled was very freeing!
