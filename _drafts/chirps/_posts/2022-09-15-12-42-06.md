When someone stands out for whatever reason and doesn’t fit the social mold and social fulfillment of the environment, they become alienated, because of misunderstanding and often no fault of their own. If people don’t know how to interact with you because of any perceived difference, whether behavioural, cultural, whatever, they’re going to tiptoe around you and eventually flat out avoid you. Focusing on doing a great job work wise isn’t enough because you often need to fulfill some social requirement, it can be more important than the actual work and job performance itself.
