What surprised me was how sad and defeated I felt -- like I was letting everyone down, like I was letting myself down. It's not surprising that a lot of the symptoms of burnout mirror many of the symptoms of depression. I felt helpless and hopeless, stopped enjoying activities I once found pleasurable. All of that. And it did NOT help to take a day or even a week off because the work was always still there. I felt like I was drowning and taking "a break" from it only made it worse. The only thing that helped was walking away -- but it takes a whole lot of privilege and access to other opportunities to be able to do that.
