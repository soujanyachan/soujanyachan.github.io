In a work setting, people tend to do things they are rewarded for, and skip tedious things if there is no immediate consequence of doing so. 

So you need to look at the incentive structures in place and figure out how and why they lead to the observed behaviour. This can be difficult to do, because the publicly stated values and incentives don't necessarily match what people actually encounter in their day-to-day work.
