Positive Affect Treatment. 

It divides joy into 3 components, past, present, and future. You sort of deal with them in the reverse order though. 

 1. Future - Pick an upcoming event. Better for it to be outside the home and/or not alone. Something small like going to a cafe, taking a walk in the park, or a little bigger like playing some board games. 

Choose 1-2 sense-based elements you think might make you happy for the event, achievable and likely, such as seeing your friends smile, hearing someone laugh at your joke, feeling the warmth of the sun, or even the taste of your coffee/pastry. 

Just something free of a lot of “ifs” and “this would make it perfect”.

 1. Present - in the moment, try to be present and look for those things. Just once. If you find yourself liking something, note it, at least mentally. If possible, note it in your phone or on paper. 

 2. Past - review the notes 1-2 days (or up to a week) later, drawing on the memory of the sensation and the resulting joy. Savor it and label it as “joyful”. 

 3. Rinse and repeat, as they say. 

Over time, if I find myself being very down or not focusing well, I’ll make a present moment game for myself. Listen for 5 sounds. Looks for all the colors in my nearby area. Or just let myself be silly and screenshot that I got 2nd place in my Star Wars co-op level.
