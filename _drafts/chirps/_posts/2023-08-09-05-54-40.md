Modern trunking refers to a virtual link between your PBX and the standard phone network (the PSTN), using an internet connection.

https://i0.wp.com/www.gradwell.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/sip-trunks-1024x683-1-1024x683-1.png

Designed to replace traditional phone lines, a SIP trunk is a virtual link between your PBX and PSTN using a broadband internet connection.
