action -> quantity -> quality -> motivation -> passion -> action

turning an interest into a passion?
- write down everything slightly interested in
	- dont edit it
- passion trial
	- pick the best one, by asking questions
	- contract to do that for one month
	- if not possible then flip a coin
	- by end of the month, action is a habit
- what if passion is wrong
	- ==indecision is not a vantage point from which you can assess your options, it's just another option==


reasons it works
- feedback loop (does not need the right action)
	- inaction feedback loop = inaction -> confusion -> fear -> inaction
- right amount of pressure
- corridor principle -> action is walking through a corridor, but staying still is just looking at it
