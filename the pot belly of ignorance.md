**If you think of your mind as a library, three things should concern you.**

1.  The information you _store_ in there — its accuracy and relevance;
2.  Your ability to find/_retrieve_ that information on demand; and
3.  Finally your ability to put that information to use when you need it — that is, you want to _apply_ it.

Clickbait media is not a nutritious diet.

**Not only do we need to** **_filter,_** **but we need to be aware of what** **_filters_** **our information has already been through.**

**a quick heuristic you can use for information you’re putting into your library**, try the two-pronged approach of:

A. Time
B. Detail.

_Time_ meaning how relevant is this historically? How long will it be accurate — what will it look like in ten minutes, ten months, ten years? If it’s going to change soon, you can probably filter it out right here.

One way to determine if the information will stand the test of time is by gauging its accuracy by examining the _details_. They are the small but powerful vitamins of your reading diet.
