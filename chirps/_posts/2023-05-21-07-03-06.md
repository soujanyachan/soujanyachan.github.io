I struggle with caring about more than one thing at any given time and it makes a career in engineering impossibly difficult. I have many of the same performance issues you have. Things are either everything or nothing to me. The things I encounter at work are never enough to engage me or make me care, so I struggle incredibly and it never lets up. The stress is unbearable and it shuts me down outside of work too. 

I have a track record of doing an amazing job when I actually care about solving the problem. Issue is that I never truly care.
