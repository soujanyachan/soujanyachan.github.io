In the Army, officers and senior NCOs learn about the 90-day (or 3x30) rule when taking over a unit/organization: - 30 days to learn about the organization, its mission, its strengths, and its shortcomings. Talk to people, especially lower-echelon leaders and specialists. - 30 days to assess necessary changes and working with key stakeholders to ensure they don't mess things up or add to other problems. - 30 days to roll out changes, understanding that people need to adapt or that established systems need to be modified to support the new policies.
