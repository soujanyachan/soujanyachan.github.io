Do what you love and what you love will become associated with everything you hate about your job, and you won’t enjoy it anymore. Do what you love and you probably won’t make much money, because guess what? A lot of other people love it to. Do what you love and you sacrifice other parts of your life to do it, and you resent the thing that you loved for making you sacrifice those things. And even if you love everything about your job, there are always days where it’s just work. You just have to put in the time and effort and you won’t want to.
