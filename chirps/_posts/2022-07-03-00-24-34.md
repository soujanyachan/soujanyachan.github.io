For more on the topic, I highly recommend "Reaching the Animal Mind" by Karen Pryor. Also, "Canine Body Language" by Brenda Aloff. And anything with Ken Ramirez (many books, articles, videos, podcasts) talking about teaching imitation and concepts (for example, teaching guide dogs about the concept of "overhead obstacle"). Also, the work of Claudia Fugazza (teaching dogs to learn novel tasks by copying humans).
