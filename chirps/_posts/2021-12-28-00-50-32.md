The Foundation series was a good reimagining of Asimov's work. Although it's been too long since I've read the few novels I have within the series, I loved the animation and the performances of Brother Day, Demerzel and the impostor Dawn, that whole arc was touching and realistic. Gaal was slightly cardboard and Salvor even more so, but the story kept my attention and I'm looking forward to the next season. 

Rating: 6.75/10
