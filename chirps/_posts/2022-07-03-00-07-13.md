How to become a senior engineer:

⚫ instead of whining, offer solutions

deliver value

• unblock your colleagues, become the linchpin of the team; read the book The Linchpin

put yourself in uncomfortable situations. Example: when an exception is thrown, you're the first to get an e-mail

• write and maintain technical documentation for the team; bring up documentation as part of every story, and you take that subtask, make it part of your team's definition of done, if not there already

really know shit. Know the terminology and how things work in a broader scope: the kernel, the networking, shell commands, Linux management, devops stuff, all kind of tools and libraries and when to use what, and when not to use something

strive to simplify, instead of over-abstract. A senior programmer makes beauty through simplicity.
