 1. Which industry/domain the startup is working in(Crunchbase)

 2. When was their latest funding(Crunchbase)

 3. How are their key metrics growing(user, adoption, expansion..)(ask this during interview)

 4. What is/are the problems they are trying to solve(Company website, ask this during interview)

 5. Whats the pedigree(college an past companies) of the people working in their team(you can find this on LinkedIn)

 6. Most importantly, if one of the founders is a techie (software/AI), it is a strong indicator that the company's problem-solving approach will be tech/AI first rather than operations first. When opposed to growing through operations, the tech/AI approach to problem solving makes business scalability extremely quick and straightforward.
