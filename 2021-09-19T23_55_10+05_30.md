"high-context" and "low-context" cultures or communication might be helpful terms for searching. (In high-context cultures, everyone shares a lot of similar background, so people can assume a lot of unspoken things -- like we call "guess" cultures here on MeFi. Whereas in low-context cultures, there's little shared assumption, so you have to be much more direct -- what we call "ask.")
